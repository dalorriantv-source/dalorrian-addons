# Dalorrian's Loot Rolls

Clean Need, Greed and Pass bars for WoW: Forever, beside your character instead of on top of it.

- **Shift-drag any roll bar to move them anywhere.** The spot is saved.
- `/rolls right` or `/rolls left` puts them on that side of your character.
- Hover the item for its tooltip, shift-click to link it, ctrl-click to try it on.
- A gold edge marks Bind on Pickup items, and the game still asks before you roll on them.
- `/rolls test` shows sample rolls (nothing is really rolled), `/rolls clear` closes them.
- `/rolls` lists every option. `/rolls off` brings back the game's own roll windows.
